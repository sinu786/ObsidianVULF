
**Your menu shouldn’t make guests guess.**

We built a 3D AR menu for restaurants. Guests scan a QR code, and every dish appears on their table at true scale. It opens straight in the phone browser, so there’s no app to download and no sign-up.

Built with WebXR and WebGL, it’s designed to do three things:

🍽️ Show guests exactly what they’re ordering, portion size and plating included  
📈 Make dishes easier to choose, which can lift orders on signature items  
🌍 Cut the language barrier for tourists and international guests
