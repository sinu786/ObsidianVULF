
A boy opens his eyes, he is struggling to fight the disorientation and finds blood seeping out of bodies, brutally massacred. their spines sticking out of their bodies. He recognizes the body. He calls. Mom.....  Dad.......

and he slowly looks behind him with absolute terror. A figure, red like the blood and towering like a god, no a demon. 

he runs away disoriented. The village burnt. He is constantly blacking in and out, seeing only parts of the vision of himself escaping. His terror flashes him the face of the figure.
